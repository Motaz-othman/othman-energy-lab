How the Ride-Through Tank works

The Ride-Through Tank sizes a chilled-water tank that keeps a data center cooled while the generators start and the chillers restart after a power failure. Enter the cooling load and the ride-through time; it returns the tank volume, the number and shape of tanks, and an indicative cost.

Method

  • Stored cooling = load × ride-through time.
  • Volume = stored cooling ÷ (4,180 kJ/m³·K × supply/return ΔT × tank efficiency), plus a safety margin.
  • Tanks are shaped up to the height limit with height 1–4 times the diameter for good stratification; larger volumes are split into equal tanks.

Frequently asked questions

How big a tank do I need to ride through a chiller restart?

For a 20 MW load, 10 minutes, a 10 K supply/return difference and 85% tank efficiency: 3,333 kWh of cooling, 338 m³ net, about 405 m³ with a 20% margin.

How many minutes of ride-through should a data center tank give?

About 10 minutes with quick-restart chillers (typical data center specification), 15–20 minutes with standard chillers, and 15 minutes for Tier IV or liquid cooling with little thermal mass.

What else must be on UPS?

The chilled-water pumps and the CDUs or air handlers, or the tank cannot deliver its cooling during the gap.
