CSV format

One row per hour, 8,760 rows from 1 January 00:00, with a header row:

Month,Day,Hour,DryBulb_C,RH_pct,Pressure_Pa
1,1,0,17.1,64.9,101205
1,1,1,15.9,70.0,101205
  • Required: dry-bulb (°C) and one of RH (%), dew point (°C) or wet-bulb (°C).
  • Optional: month (1–12); station pressure (Pa, hPa or kPa), otherwise sea level is assumed.
  • Comma, semicolon or tab separated. Headers are matched by name: “dry”/“DB”, “RH”/“humid”, “dew”, “wet”/“WB”, “month”, “press”.
EPW files for most locations are free at climate.onebuilding.org.
