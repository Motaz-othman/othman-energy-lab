Data Centers

Cooling is the largest energy use in a data center after the IT load itself. We help engineers and clients choose the right cooling plant for each data hall, starting from the ASHRAE 90.4 and 90.1 requirements and showing how far a practical design can go beyond them.

Peak Shaving Tank

The cooling plant is rated for the extreme hottest hour at full IT load, which rarely happens. A chilled-water tank covers those hours and recharges over the night, so the chillers and their electrical supply can be rated lower. Each design temperature is checked against a heatwave and the night recharge, and the recommendation keeps a safety margin.
