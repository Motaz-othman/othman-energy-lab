CDU response

The MCV is the CDU control valve on the facility water side of its heat exchanger. Defaults are on the safe side (standard actuator and pump ramp); enter the vendor values when known.
Customize
Flow margin: operating flow above the minimum the platform needs; the CDU heat exchanger is taken with the same margin. Air temperature rise only sets the airflow for the air share of the heat. Buffer at the chips: coolant in the cold plates plus the cold-plate metal, as litres of water (the manifolds do not count). Volumes are estimates; use the rack and CDU data when known.
