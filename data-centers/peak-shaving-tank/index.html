Peak Shaving Tank

A cooling plant is rated for the extreme hottest hour at full IT load, a condition that rarely happens. A chilled-water tank covers those few hours, so the chillers and their electrical supply can be rated lower. The tank recharges during the 12-hour night.

Load and plant

The tank recharges at night, 19:00 to 07:00, with the chiller capacity the IT load leaves free. The lower the night load, the faster it recharges. By day the IT load is taken at 100%.
Air-cooled chillers are the usual case: their power peaks hardest in the extreme hours.
