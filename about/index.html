Vision and mission

Where we are going, and how

Vision

Every critical and high-demand building running on the right system: less energy, lower cost and a cleaner planet, beyond the code and conventional design, and proven in numbers.

Mission

Guide engineers and clients to choose the right system for every project, with code-based analysis that shows how much energy and cost a practical design can save beyond the minimum.

Code-basedEvery number traces back to a published standard or reference method.
PracticalSavings that real equipment and real sites can deliver, not theoretical maximums.
TransparentAssumptions, limits and methods are shown, so results can be checked.

Approach

How we help you choose the right system

Every project starts from the code and ends with a clear, defensible choice.

1

Understand the project

Loads, supply temperatures, site climate, and the water and grid constraints that shape the options.

2

Start from the code baseline

ASHRAE 90.1 and 90.4 requirements, and the LEED baseline, set the recognised reference point.

3

Compare the real options

Hour by hour over a full year: energy, water, peak demand, running cost and initial cost, side by side.

4

Recommend the right system

The practical option that goes furthest beyond the minimum, with the reasons and assumptions shown.

Standards

Standards and references

The standards our analysis is built on. Planned items are on the roadmap.

ASHRAE 90.1In use

Energy Standard for Buildings. Minimum chiller efficiencies (prescriptive path, Path A).

ASHRAE 90.4In use

Energy Standard for Data Centers. Mechanical load component (MLC).

ASHRAE Handbook: FundamentalsIn use

Psychrometrics: saturation pressure, humidity ratio, wet-bulb.

AHRI 550/590In use

Chiller rating conditions for rated efficiency.

EnergyPlus / DOE-2In use

Chiller part-load performance curves.

EPW / TMY weather dataIn use

Hourly climate files for design-grade annual results.

ASHRAE 90.4 MLC limitsPlanned

Comparing each design with the maximum MLC allowed for its climate zone.

LEED (USGBC)Planned

Energy performance against the ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G baseline and LEED point thresholds.
