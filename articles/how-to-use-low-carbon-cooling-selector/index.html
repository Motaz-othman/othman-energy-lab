How to use the Low-Carbon Cooling Selector

By Eng. Mutaz Othman · 24 November 2026

The Low-Carbon Cooling Selector finds the data center cooling system with the lowest carbon and gives its yearly emissions in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent. It adds three sources: the electricity the cooling plant uses, the energy needed to supply its water, and the refrigerant that leaks from the chillers.

1. Choose the location

Pick the city. Its grid emission factor and water supply energy fill in automatically: US cities use the EPA eGRID subregion rates, Sydney the Australian National Greenhouse Accounts factors, other countries Our World in Data / Ember. Type your own values to replace them, for example a green-power contract.

2. Compare systems or enter your own numbers

  • Compare systems: enter the cooling load, average load and liquid-cooled share. The tool runs five cooling plants over the city's measured weather year, the same model as the Cooling Plant Selector.
  • My own numbers: enter the yearly cooling electricity, water use and refrigerant charge of your system, from the design, a utility bill or the BMS.

3. Set the refrigerant

Choose the refrigerant and its yearly leak rate, and the charge per kW for water-cooled and air-cooled chillers. Low-GWP refrigerants such as R-1234ze(E) remove almost all of the leakage share.

4. Read the results

One card per system shows its yearly CO₂, lowest first, with a bar split by source. The chart compares the sources side by side, and the table at the bottom lists electricity, water, charge and CO₂ for every system, with the total over the chosen period.

Frequently asked questions

Which part of a cooling system's CO₂ is largest?

Electricity, in almost every case: usually 85–99% of the total. Refrigerant leakage can matter with high-GWP refrigerants such as R-134a, and water matters in the Gulf, where desalinated water takes about 3.5 kWh per m³.

Where do the grid factors come from?

US cities use the EPA eGRID2023 subregion rates; Sydney uses the Australian National Greenhouse Accounts factors (NSW 2025–26); other countries use Our World in Data / Ember, latest year. You can type your own factor, for example a green-power contract.

Does it include the IT equipment?

No. Only the cooling system: chillers, heat-rejection fans, pumps, CRAH, fan walls and CDUs. IT electricity and UPS losses are not included.
