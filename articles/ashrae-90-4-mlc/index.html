ASHRAE 90.4 MLC explained, with the limits by climate zone

By Eng. Mutaz Othman · 22 November 2026

ASHRAE Standard 90.4, the energy standard for data centers, limits how much energy the cooling plant may use per unit of IT energy. That ratio is the mechanical load component (MLC).

Definition

Annualized MLC = annual mechanical (cooling) energy ÷ annual IT energy

Mechanical energy covers the chiller compressors, heat-rejection fans, pumps and the air handlers or CDUs. An MLC of 0.06 means 0.06 kWh of cooling energy per kWh of IT energy.

Climate zones follow ASHRAE Standard 169. Dubai and Riyadh are hot-dry zones (0B and 1B); Mumbai and Singapore are hot-humid (0A).

Typical values

For a 20 MW hall with a 30 / 40 °C liquid loop, water-cooled chillers with cooling towers and free cooling reach an annual MLC of about 0.04–0.07 across hot and temperate cities: well under the limits. Air-cooled chillers without free cooling in a hot climate are typically 0.15–0.17. Liquid cooling at warm supply temperatures is what makes low MLC possible: more free-cooling hours and a smaller chiller lift.
