How to size a chilled-water ride-through tank for a data center

By Eng. Mutaz Othman · 22 November 2026

When the grid fails, the generators start within seconds, but the chillers need minutes to restart and reach full capacity. A chilled-water ride-through tank carries the cooling load through that gap. This article shows how to size it; the free Ride-Through Tank tool does the calculation for any load.

The equation

The tank stores cooling as the temperature difference between the chilled-water supply and return:

Volume (m³) = Load (kW) × time (h) × 3,600 / (4,180 × ΔT × η) × (1 + margin)

  • 4,180 kJ/m³·K: the heat capacity of water.
  • ΔT: supply / return difference, e.g. 10 K for a 30 / 40 °C loop.
  • η: tank efficiency, about 85% for a well-stratified tank (mixing at the thermocline).
  • Margin: 20% is a reasonable allowance.

Worked example: 20 MW for 10 minutes

Cooling to store = 20,000 kW × 10 / 60 h = 3,333 kWh. Net volume = 3,333 × 3,600 / (4,180 × 10 × 0.85) = 338 m³. With a 20% margin: about 405 m³.

How many minutes?

StepTypical time
Generator start and transfer (NFPA 110 Level 1: power within 10 s)10–30 s
Loads restored in stages: pumps first, then chillers30–60 s
Chiller restart to full capacity, quick-restart chillers3–5 min
Chiller restart to full capacity, standard chillers10–15 min

Design for about 10 minutes with quick-restart chillers, 15–20 minutes with standard chillers, and 15 minutes for Tier IV or liquid cooling with little thermal mass. The margin covers a generator that does not sync at the first attempt or chillers restarted one after another.

Tank shape

Stratification works best when the tank is tall: a height of 1 to 4 times the diameter, up to the site height limit (often about 15 m). Larger volumes are split into equal tanks.

What must be on UPS

The chilled-water pumps and the CDUs or air handlers must run on UPS, or the tank cannot deliver its cooling. With liquid cooling the racks hold very little heat: even 30–60 seconds without flow can trip the IT, so continuous circulation matters as much as the tank volume.

Frequently asked questions

How big a tank do I need to ride through a chiller restart?

For a 20 MW load, 10 minutes, a 10 K supply/return difference and 85% tank efficiency: 3,333 kWh of cooling, 338 m³ net, about 405 m³ with a 20% margin.

How many minutes of ride-through should a data center tank give?

About 10 minutes with quick-restart chillers (typical data center specification), 15–20 minutes with standard chillers, and 15 minutes for Tier IV or liquid cooling with little thermal mass.

What else must be on UPS?

The chilled-water pumps and the CDUs or air handlers, or the tank cannot deliver its cooling during the gap.
